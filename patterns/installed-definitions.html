Pattern

Installed definitions

An atmosphere produces feelings to be processed. A definition produces a rule that keeps operating regardless of feeling.

Definition

Some harm doesn't operate as an ongoing feeling at all. It operates as a definition — a rule about what abuse is, what one fundamentally is, what care looks like, what love costs, what asking someone for something costs — installed once, early, by whoever held authority over a person's self-understanding before there was any competing account to check it against.

The distinction matters because the two respond to completely different work. An atmosphere generates feelings that can be processed, grieved, and that genuinely fade with attention and time. A definition isn't a feeling. It's the operating premise sitting underneath many different feelings, and it keeps functioning as true regardless of present emotional state — a person can feel entirely calm and safe and still automatically apply a rule that says love always costs something.

The mechanism

Installed early, by the only available authority

A child has no competing definition to measure it against, so whatever the primary authority defines as true about care or love becomes the operating baseline — not a claim that was ever evaluated and accepted.

Operates as a rule, not a feeling

An atmosphere's feelings fade with distance. A definition doesn't fade — it keeps running as an unquestioned premise in new situations, long after the person who installed it is gone.

Can't be felt through, only replaced

Processing the feelings attached to the experience that installed a definition doesn't touch the definition itself, because a definition was never a feeling to begin with.

What it produces over time

A person can do extensive, genuine emotional work — real grief, real anger, real lament — and still find an old definition firing automatically in a completely new relationship years later. That's not evidence the earlier work failed. It's evidence that processing and replacing are two different kinds of work, aimed at two different kinds of things, and that only one of them was actually being done.

Where this shows up

Most often clustered around a small number of foundational categories — what counts as abuse, what one fundamentally is, what care looks like, what love costs, what asking of someone costs — each typically installed during the years before any other adult's account was available for comparison.
