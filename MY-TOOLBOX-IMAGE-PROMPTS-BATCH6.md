# My Toolbox — Image Prompt Sheet, Batch 6

Six images, for the handoff-driven additions: two patterns on the
attachment mechanism, one dynamic, and the three-way deprogramming split.
Same conventions throughout: 1024×572, house style below, straight into
`assets/img/`.

## House style preamble

> A soft, atmospheric oil painting on canvas in a classical devotional style. No
> dramatic contrast or sharp realism — everything softened into a painterly,
> dreamlike atmosphere. Muted earth tones with gentle, luminous light; warm
> creams, soft blues, and golden undertones, desaturated and harmonious. Subtle
> canvas texture, smooth blended brushstrokes, no harsh edges. Diffused, glowing
> light creating a calm, contemplative mood. The composition feels symbolic and
> reverent rather than sharply detailed or photorealistic. No text, no
> lettering, no watermarks, no faces in close-up. Wide horizontal composition.

Same two carry-over rules: no faces in close-up, every image keeps a
visible light source. **Extra emphasis on zero text this round** — the
contingent-worth reroll a few batches back is the reminder why. Where a
prompt below mentions a plate, tag, or marker, it's specified worn or
blank on purpose; if a generation adds legible words anywhere, it needs a
reroll, no exceptions.

## Patterns

### `pattern-intermittent-reinforcement.jpg`
> A wind chime hanging from a porch beam in a gust of wind — its strands
> catching the movement brightly, while nearby ribbons of fabric tied
> to the same beam hang still in that same moment, from that same wind.

*Why:* the same real wind producing two different results at once, with
no way to predict which ribbon moves next — not staged, not fake, just
genuinely unpredictable.

### `pattern-trauma-bonding.jpg`
> A wooden yoke resting across two furrows in a field, one side still
> fastened in place, the other lying open and unlatched beside it.

*Why:* draws directly on the page's own closing verse — a yoke that
still holds, and the visible latch that could release it.

### `pattern-installed-definitions.jpg`
> A brass plate mounted on an old wooden door, its face worn perfectly
> smooth and blank by years of hands touching the same spot, fresh paint
> carefully worked around it rather than over it.

*Why:* blank on purpose — no engraved words, just the wear pattern of
something touched so often it shaped everything painted around it
since. Confirm the plate reads as smooth metal, not as a sign with
letters.

### `pattern-the-interval-problem.jpg`
> A wooden plank footbridge with a single board recently removed, the
> gap not yet replaced, the far bank clearly visible in daylight through
> the missing space.

*Why:* the old structure is gone and the far side is visible — but
there's nothing yet to actually stand on in between.

## Dynamics

### `dynamic-demand-that-refuses-to-be-met.jpg`
> A single paid receipt, stamped and dated, resting in an otherwise
> empty wooden collection tray, with a fresh unpaid slip already placed
> on top of it.

*Why:* the remedy delivered and set down, immediately followed by the
next demand — visualized as a stack, not a dispute. Keep both slips
blank or illegibly marked; this is the one page on this list where even
a stamp shape needs to stay abstract, not a legible word.

## Modalities

### `modality-deprogramming.jpg`
> A worn pair of work gloves set aside on a wooden bench, a fresh new
> pair resting beside them in the same light, neither one yet fully
> swapped for the other.

*Why:* put off, put on — both pairs visible at once, mid-exchange,
matching the page's own Ephesians close.
